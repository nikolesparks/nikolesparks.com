# Keyword Reference: nikolesparks.com
Standing keyword research across every audience the practice serves. Read this before briefing or drafting a post so the angle is chosen against real demand rather than guesswork, and add to it whenever a new pull happens.
Source: Opinly `search_keywords` and `get_keyword_gap`, US national market, pulled 2026-09-13. Companion file: `keyword-reference.xlsx` (same data, sortable and filterable).
---
## How to read this
**Volume** is estimated US searches per month. A blank means Opinly returned no figure for that exact phrase, which is not the same as zero demand; treat those as unknown rather than dead.
**KD** is difficulty from 0 to 100. For a site this size, under 15 is realistically winnable, 15 to 35 is a medium-term target, and above 40 is a long game that depends on domain authority the site doesn't have yet.
**Maps to** is the page or post that should own the term. "Unclaimed" means nothing on the site targets it.
A useful sort is volume divided by difficulty. The spreadsheet has that as an opportunity score.
**CPC**, where the gap analysis reports it, is the best available proxy for commercial intent. A $17 cost per click means advertisers are competing for people who book; $0.10 means students and the curious. High volume plus low CPC builds authority; low volume plus high CPC fills a calendar.
### Two limitations to keep in mind
**Local terms are not available.** Only the US national market is configured in Opinly for this account. Geo-modified pulls for Newport Beach, Fullerton, Orange County and Long Beach came back as national noise rather than local volume, so local demand is not in this document. Google Search Console remains the source of truth for local performance, and the striking-distance table in `content-strategy.md` is built from it.
**Opinly's expansion drifts on abstract seeds.** Seeds like "creative burnout" and "faith based therapy" returned unrelated results (physical therapy, Stockholm syndrome, church directories). Concrete multi-word seeds work far better. When a cluster below looks thin, that usually reflects the tool rather than the market.

## Suggested additions to the roadmap
Based on demand rather than the existing plan, these are the highest-value posts that don't exist yet:
**People-pleasing** (12,000 combined, KD 0). Compliance, the false self, and what it costs.
**Breakups** (49,500, KD 0). Grief-adjacent, and the grief post already proves the site can rank in this territory.
**Infidelity in couples work** (60,000 combined across two phrasings, KD 0).
**Expanding the attachment post to own "avoidant attachment treatment"** (40,500, KD 9). Cheapest win here: the page exists.
**A first-therapy post** covering how to start, what to talk about, and what to expect.
**A teen service page.** Not a blog post, but 2,000 a month at KD 0 with nowhere to land.
**A defense-mechanisms cluster** (intellectualization, introjection, and related), from the competitor gap: roughly 50,000 monthly searches under KD 10, serving authority rather than bookings.
**Adult friendship rupture** ($10 to $17 CPC at KD 0, and genuinely underserved).
**Marriage repair language** ("how to save a marriage" and its variants, 4,500 combined at KD 0 to 9).
